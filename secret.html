To my dear Shayma,

I want to begin by saying this is not a love letter.
I also don’t quite know what kind of letter it is.

If I had to name it, I would call it a letter of potential letting go.

That said… you are the most beautiful creature in all of God’s creation. There was no before you, and there will be no after you.

No mortal soul comes close to your being; they are all words, while you… you are poetry.

Do not worry. I will never blame you for not liking me. If I were to meet myself, I wouldn’t like me either.

There may be an infinite amount of hope in the universe, but not for you and me. Not in this life.

I knew from the moment I met you that I would spend a lifetime missing you. And even though I’ve waited many lifetimes already—what’s one more?

Deep down, I know you are a language I’m no longer fluent in. Still, I remember how to read it.

I hope you find someone who speaks you effortlessly, so you don’t have to spend a lifetime translating your soul.

I knew from the first hesitation that this road… your road… wasn’t built for me. I walked it anyway. And if I had the chance to do it all again, I wouldn’t change a damn thing.

You were no mistake.

Sometimes I think, I can’t wait to tell you this and that. Then I remember… you left, even while you were there. And I lost my best friend.

Still, I smile, because at least we were under the same sky.

I’m over it.
I’m over it.
I’m fucking over it.
I swear I am.
But it still stings from time to time.

The best way I can explain it is this:
it’s like a missing tooth. You know it’s gone—gone for good. Yet your tongue still searches for it.

Replaceable, but unreplaceable.

If it were my tongue, I would have cut it off. But it wasn’t. It was my heart.

Even if I don’t know what we are now, I know this—
I miss what we once were.

Forgive me if I failed to describe your soul accurately.
You were never meant to be described.

“The moon is beautiful… but it’s only the moon.”

This is where I stop.

Note: you're still important to me with or without conversation.
